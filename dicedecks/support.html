FAQ

  • How do I play? Open HOW TO PLAY on the title screen. Build poker sets over 12 turns; raise the risk for bigger rewards.
  • My score is not on the leaderboard. Only games started online are ranked (offline games are not). The Daily Challenge counts your first official attempt of the day.
  • Change my nickname: LEADERBOARD › NAME.
  • Delete my account: LEADERBOARD › Delete my account removes your nickname and scores from our server. See the privacy policy.
  • Restore a purchase (when purchases are added): use the Restore button in the shop, or write to us.
