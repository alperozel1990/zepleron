Dice & Decks — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Developer: Zepleron (Alper Özel) · App: com.zepleron.dicedecks

In short: this version collects no personal data, shares nothing and never connects to the internet.

  • There are no accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics or crash reporting.
  • Your game progress, best scores and sound setting stay on your device, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • The only permission used is vibration (touch feedback).
  • The app is not directed at children under 13.

If online leaderboards, ads or purchases are added later, this page will be updated and you will be told in the app beforehand.
